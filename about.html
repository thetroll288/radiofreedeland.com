Read this first: none of this is real.

Radio Free DeLand is a fictional radio station and a work of entertainment. Everything you hear here — the scripts, the voices, and the entire cast — is generated by artificial intelligence.

The full disclosure

  • Scripts are AI-written. Every episode script — monologues, caller dialogue, sponsor reads, station IDs — is written by an AI system from that day’s real news headlines.
  • Voices are AI-synthesized. Every voice you hear is produced by text-to-speech AI. No human speaks on this station.
  • The cast is fictional. Mack Danner, Dana Cruz, Carla, Sam Reyes, and every caller are invented characters. They are not real people, and any resemblance to a real person is coincidence.
  • Callers, sponsors, and opinions are made up. The news topics come from real headlines; the people arguing about them, the local businesses they mention, and the views they express are fiction.

What the station is

Radio Free DeLand is an automated station out of DeLand, Florida — “The Heart and Soul of Volusia County.” New episodes are written, voiced, and published automatically, and this site keeps the complete archive: every program that has ever aired, newest first, free to play.

The shows

  • The Danner Line with Mack Danner — weekday-morning call-in. Sharp, sarcastic, acerbic; conservative-leaning but unpredictable. Produced by Carla.
  • Loud & Clear with Dana Cruz — late-morning call-in. Progressive, warm but relentless; a DeLand native who dismantles weak arguments kindly. Produced by dry-witted Sam Reyes.

A note on the news

Episode topics are built from real headlines — state, national, international, and local DeLand/Volusia news. Facts in the scripts come from actual reporting. Everything else — who says what about those facts, and how — is invented for the show.
